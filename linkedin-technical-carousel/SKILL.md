# LinkedIn Technical Carousel Skill

This skill guides the creation of high-impact technical slide carousels for LinkedIn, focusing strictly on content structure, narrative progression, and concise copywriting.

## Core Structure & Narrative Flow
* **Hook Early (Slide 1):** Use a bold, problem-solving headline that poses a high-stakes question or presents a stark paradox.
* **One Idea Per Slide:** Dedicate exactly one core technical concept, architectural layer, or failure point per slide.
* **Progressive Disclosure:** Reveal technical complexity step-by-step across the deck (e.g., Lure → Perimeter Collapse → Technical Architecture → The Decisive Barrier → The Solution).
* **Flexible Slide Count:** Decks typically range from **4 to 8 slides** depending on topic complexity. Never pad with fluff to reach a target count, and never compress two complex ideas into one slide.
* **Strong Finish (Final Slide):** End with a single, clear Call to Action (CTA) directing readers to the full article or newsletter issue.

---

## Strict Copywriting & Text Rules
* **Max Word Count:** Limit text to **30–40 words max per slide**. Cut all filler.
* **No Walls of Text:** Break all paragraphs into clean, single-sentence bullet points.
* **Scanning:** **Bold key technical terms** so readers scanning the deck instantly grasp the core takeaway.
* **Clean Markdown Output:** Do NOT include word count annotations or unnecessary emojis in the final slide text (keep only the link indicator emoji `👉` on the CTA).

---

## Technical Content Principles
* **Analogy First:** State the conceptual problem in plain English before presenting technical mechanics or code.
* **Before / After Contrast:** Contrast the flawed assumption (Problem) with the hardened control (Solution).
* **Visual Anchors:** Use clean, minimal ASCII flowcharts or directional arrows (`↓`, `──►`) to illustrate attack chains and data flows.
